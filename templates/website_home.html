End-to-End Acoustic Intelligence Pipeline

From physical sound pressure waves to cryptographically verified threat alerts in under 350 milliseconds.

Ingestion & SHA-256

Accepts WAV/MP3/M4A or 1.5s sliding mic windows with duplicate fingerprinting.

Step 02

Pre-Processing

Resamples to 22.05 kHz mono, trims silence, checks SNR dB and clipping guards.

Step 03

Feature Extraction

Computes 128-band Mel-Spectrograms, MFCCs, Chroma, Spectral Centroid, and ZCR.

Step 04

Dual-AI Inference

Executes Python CNN & Google Teachable Machine in parallel independent passes.

Step 05

Policy & Alert Dispatch

Evaluates confidence delta, top-two margin, repeated confirmation, and triggers alerts.
