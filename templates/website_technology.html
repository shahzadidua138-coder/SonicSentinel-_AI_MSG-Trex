Pipeline Flow

End-to-End Signal Processing & Inference

Every audio buffer follows a strictly reproducible 5-stage transformation pipeline.

Stage 01

DSP Pre-Processing & Normalization

Continuous audio or uploaded files are downmixed to single-channel mono, resampled to 22,050 Hz, peak-amplitude normalized to [-1.0, 1.0], and checked against silence thresholds (< -60 dBFS) and clipping boundaries (≥ 99% full-scale).

Stage 02

373-Acoustic Feature Vector Extraction

Computes 80 MFCC descriptors (40 mean + 40 std), 256 Mel-Spectrogram bands (128 mean + 128 std), 24 Chroma features (12 mean + 12 std), Spectral Centroid, Bandwidth, Rolloff, Zero-Crossing Rate, Root-Mean-Square energy, and Onset strength.

Stage 03

Python SVM Trained Model Scoring

The 373-feature vector is scaled using the saved scaler.joblib and evaluated by our best-performing Radial Basis Function (RBF) Support Vector Machine, outputting calibrated probability distributions across all 10 sound categories.

Stage 04

Google Teachable Machine Arbitration

The audio segment is independently classified by the Google Teachable Machine neural benchmark. The arbitration engine calculates Δ = |Python - GTM| and top-two confidence margin to assign Strong Match, Acceptable Match, or Review Required.

Stage 05

Alert Rules Policy & Escalation

Matches detection against the active JSON alert policy rules. Enforces consecutive window confirmation for critical events (Gunshot, Scream) and triggers notifications, email alerts, and audit logs.

Stage 06

SQLite WAL Persistence & PDF Export

Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) ensures high concurrency under heavy write loads. Allows instant generation of cryptographic PDF incident certificates with embedded waveforms and audit details.

Dual-Model Arbitration Formula (SRS Step 11)

Confidence Delta (Δ):
Δ = |Python_Conf - GTM_Conf|

Δ ≤ 0.05 → Strong Match
0.05 < Δ ≤ 0.15 → Acceptable Match
Δ > 0.15 → Weak Match

Top-Two Margin Threshold:
Margin = Top1_Conf - Top2_Conf

Margin ≥ 0.10 → Unambiguous Prediction
Margin < 0.10 → Marked Uncertain • Sent to Review Queue
