Multi-Format Ingestion & SHA-256 Fingerprint

Accepts WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG, and M4A audio streams. Every clip generates an instant cryptographic SHA-256 hash to prevent redundant classification, detect duplicate uploads, and track file provenance across recording stations.

SRS xii • xiii • xiv

Audio Quality Gatekeeper & Noise Profiler

Evaluates incoming audio before neural scoring. Detects silent recordings (< -60 dBFS), flags severe digital clipping (≥ 99% peak), and computes real-time Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) to categorize signals as Good, Acceptable, Poor, or Unusable.

  • Silence rejection prevents false threat alerts
  • Clipping guards alert operators to microphone overload
  • Environmental background noise estimation
SRS xx

373-Dimensional Acoustic Feature Extraction

Extracts comprehensive spectral, timbral, and temporal acoustic signatures using Librosa and SciPy. Encodes 40 MFCC mean & standard deviations, 128 Mel bands, 12 Chroma pitch classes, ZCR, Spectral Rolloff, Centroid, and Onset strength.

  • 40 MFCC coefficients capture vocal and impulse formants
  • Spectral Centroid & Bandwidth isolate mechanical faults
  • Zero-Crossing Rate detects high-frequency glass shattering

Dual-AI Cross-Arbitration Engine

Cross-validates predictions between the trained Python ML model (96.44% test accuracy) and an independent Google Teachable Machine benchmark. Evaluates confidence difference Δ = |Python - GTM| and top-two margin to classify agreement.

  • Strong Match (Δ ≤ 5%), Acceptable (Δ ≤ 15%)
  • Automatic routing of Model Disagreement to Review Queue
  • Top-Two Margin thresholding prevents borderline classification

  • Configurable cooldown periods prevent alert floods
