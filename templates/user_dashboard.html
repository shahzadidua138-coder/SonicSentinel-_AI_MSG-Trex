10 Mandatory Acoustic Hazard Categories & Safety Guidelines

SRS Section 7 & 8 Acoustic Safety Taxonomy

SRS COMPLIANT
1. Gunshot CRITICAL

Sharp acoustic impulse wavefront. Evacuate immediately or seek ballistic cover.

2. Panic Scream CRITICAL

High pitch distress vocalization. Security personnel dispatched to station.

3. Person Asking Help CRITICAL

Urgent verbal request for assistance. Alert emergency medical unit.

4. Glass Breaking HIGH HAZARD

High frequency shattering. Check windows, display cases, and perimeter doors.

5. Machinery Fault HIGH HAZARD

Unbalanced mechanical friction or motor bearing failure. Maintenance alerted.

6. Alarm or Siren WARNING

Emergency tone broadcast. Verify evacuation route instructions.

7. Vehicle Horn MEDIUM

Vehicle traffic warning. Exercise caution in loading dock area.

8. Aggression / Shouting MEDIUM

Elevated vocal aggression. Facility security officer dispatch dispatched.

9. Animal Sound LOW

Domestic dog bark or wildlife sound near perimeter fence.

10. Background Noise NORMAL

HVAC, wind, office murmur. Normal operational acoustic baseline.
