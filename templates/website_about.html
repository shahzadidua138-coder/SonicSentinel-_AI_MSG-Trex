The Challenge

The Limits of Manual Audio Monitoring

Traditional security and industrial facilities rely on humans continuously listening to audio feeds or reviewing recordings after an incident has already occurred. This manual workflow is time-consuming, prone to missed cues, delayed responses, and inconsistent decisions in noisy environments.

The Solution

SonicSentinel Autonomous Defense

SonicSentinel AI continuously processes acoustic pressure waves across 10 mandatory sound categories. Utilizing dual-model cross-arbitration between a Python Support Vector Machine and Google Teachable Machine, the system delivers verified alerts in under 350 milliseconds.

Ethical AI

Competition Integrity & Anti-Shortcut

In full adherence to TechWiz 7 rules, no predictions are simulated or hardcoded. The application features fully trained model artifacts, reproducible feature extraction matrices, documented training logs, and privacy controls that prevent covert recording.
